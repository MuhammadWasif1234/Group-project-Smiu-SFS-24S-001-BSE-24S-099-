Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register for events?

All currently enrolled SMIU students with a valid roll number.

How do I start a new society?

Contact the Student Affairs Office with your proposal and a faculty advisor.

Can I cancel a registration?

Yes, email the office at least 24 hours before the event.
